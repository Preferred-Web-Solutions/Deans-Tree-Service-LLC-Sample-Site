Stump grinding

An old stump in the middle of the yard is a mower hazard and a trip hazard. Grinding takes it below the grass line so you can reseed or plant over it.

Call or text for an estimate

What this covers

  • Stumps ground below grade
  • Old stumps and fresh ones from a removal
  • Surface roots near the stump
  • Grindings raked out or hauled away
  • Several stumps in one visit

Good to know

Tell us roughly how wide the stump is across the top, and whether the grinder can get through a gate.
