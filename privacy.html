Who we are

This site is for Dean’s Tree Service, LLC, Springfield, Ohio, phone (937) 605-7544. The preview was built by Preferred Web Solutions (preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com).

What we collect

  • Browsing this site: nothing is collected by us. The site has no analytics, advertising pixels, embedded maps, videos or social widgets. Fonts and photos are hosted on this site, so your browser does not contact Google or other font services. Like any website, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and operation.
  • Your cookie choice: we save your consent choice in your browser’s local storage so we don’t ask on every page (see the Cookie Policy).
  • The quote form: this is a working demo and is not connected. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • Report a website issue form: if you use it, what you type is sent to our form processor, Formspree, so we can read it and reply.
  • Links to Google, the BBB and Facebook: these links open those companies’ own sites in a new tab. Their privacy policies apply there. Nothing from them loads on this site unless you click.

How we use it

Only to answer your message or fix a site problem you reported. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. To make a request, email preferredwebsolutions@gmail.com. You can change or withdraw your cookie choice at any time with the “Cookie settings” button in the footer.

Children

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date and version above.
