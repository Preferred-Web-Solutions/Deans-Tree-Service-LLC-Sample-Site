Everything from the crown to the stump

Five services, top of the tree to the ground. Every job ends with the yard cleaned up.

All services

  1. 01

    Trimming & pruning

    Limbs hanging over the roof, rubbing the gutters or blocking the driveway, cut back clean, with the dead wood taken out while the crew is up there.

  2. 02

    Storm & emergency cleanup

    When a storm drops a tree on the yard or leaves a limb hanging, call. Customers mention Dean’s for quick work after storm damage and a clean yard afterwards.

  3. 03

    Tree removal

    Dead, leaning or simply too-big trees, taken down safely. Big removals are what customers mention most, and the crew cleans up when the tree is gone.

  4. 04

    Stump grinding

    An old stump in the middle of the yard is a mower hazard and a trip hazard. Grinding takes it below the grass line so you can reseed or plant over it.

  5. 05

    Land & brush clearing

    Overgrown lots, fence lines swallowed by brush and back corners nobody has touched in years, cleared so you can use the land again.
