A family tree crew from Springfield

Dean’s Tree Service has been taking care of trees around Springfield since 2007.

Family owned and operated

Dean’s Tree Service started in Springfield in 2007 and became Dean’s Tree Service, LLC in 2013. Owner David Dean Jr. runs a family operation that handles everything from trimming to big removals, storm cleanup, stump grinding and land clearing.

The trucks say it plainly: free estimates, fully insured, and senior and military discounts (to be confirmed).

Customers say the crew is polite, hard working and on time, the prices are fair, and the yard is cleaned up when the job is done.
